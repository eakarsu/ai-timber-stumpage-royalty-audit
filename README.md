# Timber Stumpage Royalty Audit

Reconstruct harvested volume and contract pricing to recover underreported timber and unsupported deductions.

**Primary buyer:** Timberland owners and investment managers. **Evidence:** stumpage agreements, tract cruises, species, grades, scale tickets, mill receipts, indexes, deductions, settlements, and payments.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Stumpage agreement library
- Tract cruise inventory
- Species grade mapping
- Harvest load ingestion
- Scale ticket validation
- Mill receipt matching
- Weight conversion factors
- Board-foot calculation
- Index price validation
- Hauling deduction audit
- Road cost deduction
- Settlement recalculation
- Logger mill dispute workflow
- Cash reconciliation
- Tract species analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4711>. API: `5711`.
